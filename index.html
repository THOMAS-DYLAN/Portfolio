Why Your Own Store

The marketplace isn't yours.

Stop paying the platform tax

Marketplaces take 6–15% of every sale, plus listing and payment fees. Your own store keeps that money in your pocket — month after month.

02

Own your customers

Etsy and Amazon own your buyer list — not you. Your own store means an email list, repeat sales, and customers you can actually reach again.
