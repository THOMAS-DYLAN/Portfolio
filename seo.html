Frequently asked.

Answers to what marketplace sellers and new brands most often ask about getting their own store.

Moving off a marketplace means getting your own e-commerce website where customers buy directly from you — no platform fees, no competing listings. Deadwood Digital builds the full store (product pages, cart, checkout, inventory) on infrastructure you own, then helps you point customers there from your marketplace profile, social bio, or packaging inserts. Custom e-commerce builds start at $2,500. You keep your existing marketplace shops running during the transition if you want.

Marketplaces take 6–15% of every sale plus listing and payment fees, they own your customer list, and they can suspend your account with no warning. Your own store keeps the fees, builds an email list you control, lets you build a real brand instead of being one listing among thousands, and can never be shut down by a policy change. The marketplace is great for discovery — your own store is where you actually own the business.
