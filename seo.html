Answers to the questions small businesses most often have about getting a website built.

At Deadwood Digital, small business websites start at $250 for a single-page template site and go up to $3,000 for a fully custom 7–10 page website with a complete e-commerce suite. Template sites (Starter $250, Standard $500, Premium $1,000) are faster and more affordable. Custom builds (Foundation $500, Staple $1,500, Signature $3,000) are designed from scratch around your brand and include e-commerce capability.

A template website is built on a pre-designed framework that gets customized for your business — it's faster and more affordable. A custom website is built from a blank page, with every layout, color, font, and structure chosen specifically for your brand. Custom builds also include e-commerce capability and tend to stand out more from competitors. Both are professionally designed, mobile responsive, and deployed ready to go live.

A single-page template site (Starter, $250) typically takes 1–3 days. A 3–5 page template site (Standard, $500) takes 3–6 days. Larger template or custom builds take 1–4 weeks depending on scope. Timeline depends on how quickly client content and feedback is provided.

Social media pages are rented space — the platform controls your reach, your audience, and the rules. A website is owned space. It's where customers go to find your hours, services, prices, and contact info without an algorithm deciding whether to show it to them. A website also shows up in Google search results, which social media profiles don't fully replace. At minimum, a single-page site ($250) gives you a professional home base that you control.

Getting indexed by Google requires a few things: submitting your sitemap to Google Search Console, having proper title tags and meta descriptions on every page, building your site with clean semantic HTML, and adding structured data (schema markup) so Google understands your business type and services. Shopify, Wix, and Squarespace charge monthly fees ($25–$300/month) and take a percentage of every sale. A custom e-commerce site built by Deadwood Digital is a one-time fee — you own the code, there are no platform fees, and you're not locked into any ecosystem.
